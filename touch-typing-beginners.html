Touch Typing for Beginners

Learn the basics of touch typing and build better keyboard habits.

What Is Touch Typing?

Touch typing is a typing technique that allows you to type without constantly looking at the keyboard. Instead of searching for individual keys, you learn the position of the keys and use your fingers consistently.

The goal is to make common keyboard movements more familiar so you can focus on the text you are typing rather than finding each key.

Learn the Home Row

The home row is the middle row of letters on a standard QWERTY keyboard. It provides a starting position for your fingers when learning touch typing.

The left hand typically rests around the A, S, D, and F keys, while the right hand rests around the J, K, L, and semicolon keys. The F and J keys usually have small raised markers that can help you find the correct hand position without looking down.

Use Consistent Finger Placement

Touch typing generally assigns different groups of keys to specific fingers. This allows your fingers to return to familiar positions after pressing a key.

At first, this may feel slower than using whichever finger reaches a key most easily. With practice, consistent finger placement can become more natural and reduce the need to visually search for keys.

Reduce Looking at the Keyboard

One of the main goals of touch typing is to become less dependent on looking at the keyboard. When practicing, try to keep your attention on the text rather than checking the location of every key.

It is normal to make mistakes while learning. Accuracy and familiarity should come before trying to achieve a high typing speed.

Practice Common Words

Start with simple words and common letter combinations. Repeated practice helps you become more familiar with the movements required to type frequently used patterns.

As you become more comfortable, gradually introduce longer words, punctuation, and more varied text. The aim is to build consistent typing habits rather than simply memorise a single set of words.

Prioritise Accuracy

Beginners often try to increase their typing speed too quickly. This can make mistakes more frequent and reinforce incorrect typing habits.

Instead, focus on pressing the correct keys consistently. As your keyboard familiarity improves, your typing speed can increase naturally through continued practice.
