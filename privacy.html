Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to KeyGati. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and handled when you visit or use the KeyGati website and its typing tests.

KeyGati is designed as a simple typing practice and testing website. We aim to collect only information that is necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the website.

2. Information We Collect

KeyGati does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the typing tests.

Depending on the services enabled on the website, limited technical and usage information may be collected automatically. This may include information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, approximate location, referral information, and general website usage information.

3. Typing Test Data

KeyGati's typing tests may calculate information such as typing speed, accuracy, elapsed time, and character statistics while you use a test.

These results are primarily used to display your performance during the test. KeyGati does not require you to provide your name, email address, or account information to perform a typing test.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

KeyGati may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage, maintain services, and display advertisements.

Different types of technologies may be used for different purposes. For example, necessary technologies may support website functionality, while analytics technologies may help us understand how visitors use the website. Advertising technologies may be used by third-party advertising providers to serve and measure advertisements.

Third-party services used on KeyGati may also place or access cookies or similar technologies on your device. These services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Advertising

KeyGati may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to KeyGati or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on a user's visits to this website and/or other websites on the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings .

If other third-party advertising providers or ad networks are used on KeyGati, they may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, or personalize advertisements. Their practices are governed by their respective privacy policies and may provide additional options for managing personalized advertising.

6. Analytics

KeyGati may use third-party analytics services to understand how visitors use the website, identify technical issues, and improve the user experience.

Analytics providers may collect information such as pages visited, browser type, device information, approximate location, referral information, and general website usage patterns.

Analytics services may use cookies or similar technologies to collect this information. The collection and use of information by these services are subject to their respective privacy policies.

7. Third-Party Links and Services

KeyGati may contain links to third-party websites or services. KeyGati is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the operation of KeyGati. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

KeyGati is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through the website.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as KeyGati develops or as legal, technical, or operational requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or KeyGati's privacy practices, please visit the About page for contact information.
