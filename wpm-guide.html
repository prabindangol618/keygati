What Is WPM?

Understanding words per minute and how typing speed is measured.

What Does WPM Mean?

WPM stands for words per minute. It is one of the most common measurements used to describe typing speed. A typing speed of 60 WPM, for example, means that a person's typing performance is equivalent to typing 60 standard words in one minute.

WPM provides a simple way to compare typing performance across different typing tests. Because individual words can have different lengths, typing tests use a standard definition of a word rather than counting every actual word separately.

How Is WPM Calculated?

A standard typing calculation treats five characters as one word. Characters include letters, numbers, spaces, and punctuation. This makes the measurement more consistent across different pieces of text.

The basic calculation is:

WPM = Correct Characters ÷ 5 ÷ Time in Minutes

For example, if you correctly type 300 characters in three minutes, your typing speed would be:

300 ÷ 5 ÷ 3 = 20 WPM

Why Are Five Characters Counted as One Word?

Actual words vary significantly in length. A short word such as "cat" contains only three letters, while a longer word can contain many more. Counting actual words would therefore make comparisons between different texts less consistent.

Using a standard five-character word makes typing-speed measurements more consistent. Spaces and punctuation are also included when counting characters, so the calculation represents the overall amount of text typed rather than only the letters.

Does WPM Measure Accuracy?

WPM primarily measures typing speed, so it does not tell the whole story by itself. A person may type quickly while also making many mistakes, which is why typing tests commonly measure accuracy separately.

Looking at both WPM and accuracy provides a more useful view of typing performance. Improving typing speed is generally more useful when it is accompanied by consistent and accurate typing.

How Can You Improve Your WPM?

Improving typing speed usually requires consistent practice rather than simply trying to type as fast as possible. Learning touch-typing techniques, using correct finger placement, and becoming familiar with the keyboard can help develop more efficient typing habits.

It is also useful to focus on accuracy while practicing. Once your typing becomes more consistent, gradually increasing your speed can help improve your WPM without sacrificing accuracy.
