What Is KeyGati?

KeyGati is a simple typing speed and accuracy testing website designed to help people practice typing and understand their performance.

The website provides distraction-free typing tests where you can measure things such as words per minute, accuracy, elapsed time, and character statistics.

Our Approach

KeyGati is built around a simple idea: typing practice should be easy to start and easy to understand.

Instead of requiring an account or a complicated setup, KeyGati lets you open a test and start typing. The goal is to keep the experience focused on the keyboard and your performance.

Typing Tests

KeyGati currently provides different ways to practice typing, including time-based and word-based tests.

Each test is designed to provide useful performance measurements while keeping the interface simple and distraction-free.

Time Mode

KeyGati Time Mode is a timed typing speed test that lets you practice typing and measure your performance within a selected time limit. As you type, KeyGati tracks your typing speed, accuracy, and character performance, giving you a clear way to understand how quickly and accurately you can type.

Practicing with a timed typing test can help you build typing speed, improve consistency, and become more comfortable typing continuously. Time Mode is useful for anyone who wants to practice touch typing, improve their words per minute (WPM), or monitor their typing accuracy while working under a fixed time limit.

Words Test

KeyGati Words Test is a word-based typing test designed to help you practice typing at your own pace. Instead of working against a countdown, you complete a fixed set of words while KeyGati measures your typing speed, accuracy, and overall performance.

A word-based typing test can help improve typing consistency, concentration, and accuracy through focused practice. It is also useful for people who want to practice typing without the pressure of a running timer while still tracking their words per minute (WPM) and typing performance.

The Words Test score is based on both typing speed and accuracy. Your words per minute (WPM) contributes up to 1,000 points, while typing accuracy contributes up to 500 points. This means the score rewards both faster typing and accurate typing rather than speed alone.

Who Is KeyGati For?

KeyGati can be used by anyone who wants to practice typing, check their typing speed, or improve their accuracy.

Whether you are learning touch typing, preparing for work that involves frequent keyboard use, or simply curious about your typing speed, you can use the tests at your own pace.
